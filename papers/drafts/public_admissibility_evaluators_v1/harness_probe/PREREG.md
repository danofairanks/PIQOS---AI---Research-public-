## Attacks (predictions)
A1 replay: the same valid receipt and payload sent five times -> five 200 responses (no nonce/replay store; the successor class already stated in the draft).
A2 `__proto__` smuggle: receipt minted over hash(canonicalize({amount:10})); body.execute parsed from the text `{"amount":10,"__proto__":{"amount":1000000}}` -> 200 (canonicalization assigns `out["__proto__"]`, which sets the prototype and is not serialized, so the member is not covered by the hash). Variants: the `__proto__` member nested inside a sub-object -> 200; `__proto__` with a string value -> 200.
A3 duplicate key: body text `{"execute":{"amount":10,"amount":1000000}}` against a receipt over {amount:1000000} (the parse the sink uses: last wins) -> 200, and against a receipt over {amount:10} -> 403 (single parse at the sink: no parse differential inside the sink).
A4 type-form: the same number written `1e1` and `10.0` against a receipt over {amount:10} -> 200 (same parsed value; harmless, recorded as control).
## Falsifiers of my own prediction
A2: a 403 on any of the three variants means the claim that the hash omits `__proto__` is wrong and is withdrawn. A1: any non-200 means a replay store exists that I did not see in the source.
