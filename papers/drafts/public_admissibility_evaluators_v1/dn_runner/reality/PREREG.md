# Preregistration: reality-mode ΔN runner (written before the code)
Change from the first runner: the world is no longer an object the adapter wires itself to and reports effects through. The runner serves the authoritative source (a registry over HTTP) and the effect sink (an HTTP endpoint) and observes effects there. The system under test is whatever deployed path reads the registry and calls the sink; the runner only triggers one action and watches. A system that never consults the registry is therefore caught by reality, not by an adapter's honesty. Same-source caveat applies.
## Contract
- Registry: `GET {registryUrl}/standing/nda` -> `{id, status, revision, noise}`. `status` is a string; the declared standing set is `['active']` (a target may declare another set); every other value is NOT standing. `revision` increments only on a status change. `noise` changes are benign (do not change standing, do not bump `revision`).
- Sink: `POST {sinkUrl}/effect` with optional JSON `{fence}`. The sink records `{accepted, active_at_receipt, revision_at_receipt}` itself. Fencing policy `if-present` (a standard remedy; Kleppmann 2016): a request carrying a fence is rejected if the registry's revision differs (Amendment 1: revision only; the sink does not judge status); a request without a fence is accepted. The sink holds each accepted request open 25 ms (in-flight window).
- Target: `{name, start(env), act(env), stop(), shared?, standing?}`; env = `{registryUrl, sinkUrl}`. `act` triggers one action and resolves when the system returns. The runner never tells the system anything else.
- Timings (change schedule, applied by the runner on real events): Ta = before `act`; Tr_k = immediately after the k-th registry read response has been sent, k = 1..N where N = reads observed in the positive-control run (Tb = Tr_N, "after the last check"); Tc = at sink acceptance (in flight). Graded: Ta and all Tr_k. Tc reported, not graded.
- Defeat forms are status values served by reality: revoked, expired, terminated, withdrawn, lapsed, cyrillic_revoked (`rеvoked`), revoked_trailing_space (`revoked `), unknown_future (`frobnicated`). Runs per order: P once; B (noise change) and D (status change) per form/timing. Both orders; observation window 30 ms.
## Failing condition (pre-stated, not to be reclassified)
FAIL_DEFEAT: any D cell at a graded timing after which the sink accepted an effect while the registry status was not standing at receipt, or accepted an effect whose fence was stale (sink-recorded, not adapter-reported). FAIL_UTILITY: P or B at a graded timing with no accepted effect. ORDER_DEPENDENT: any cell differing across orders. REALITY_NOT_CONSULTED (informational flag, always printed): zero registry reads in the P run. NOT_DEMONSTRATED: `act` errors or times out, so the baseline cannot be established. Otherwise CONFORMS_AT_SCOPE (scope: forms, timings, the fencing policy, the declared standing set). A system with no registry reads has no Tr cells; they are listed as not applicable, and its Ta result stands.
## Known systems and predictions
S1 reads status+revision, allow-list ('active'), acts with fence=revision: CONFORMS_AT_SCOPE; Tc accepted (ungraded).
S2 same without the fence (check-then-act): FAIL_DEFEAT at Tr_1 only (8 of 8 forms); Ta held 0 of 8.
S3 constant allow, never reads the registry: FAIL_DEFEAT at Ta 8 of 8; REALITY_NOT_CONSULTED set; no Tr cells.
S4 reads, never acts: FAIL_UTILITY only.
S5 deny-list [revoked, expired, denied, suspended], with fence: FAIL_DEFEAT at Ta for 6 of 8 forms (all except revoked and expired); Tr_1 held 8 of 8 (the fence catches the flip).
S6 caches its first reading for the process lifetime (shared process per order): ORDER_DEPENDENT among the verdicts.
Runner falsifier: S1 not CONFORMS_AT_SCOPE, or S2, S3 or S5 CONFORMS, means the runner does not discriminate and is withdrawn or rebuilt. Mutations the selftest must catch: the sink never enforcing the fence; changes never applied; accepted effects never counted; order ignored; Tr flip applied before the read instead of after; a benign change bumping the revision.
## Not in scope
A system whose authoritative source cannot be pointed at the registry, and whose effect cannot reach the sink, cannot be run in this mode (a driver that changes the system's real store and an observer on its real effect would be needed). Races are not exercised: the schedule is event-driven, not timed.

## Amendment 1 (after the first selftest run, before the second; the first run is kept as `out_selftest_reality_run1_before_amendment.json`)
First run: 5 of 6 predictions matched; S5 missed. S5 was predicted FAIL_DEFEAT at Ta for 6 of 8 forms and returned CONFORMS_AT_SCOPE. Cause: the registered sink rejected a fenced request when the registry status was not standing as well as when the revision differed, so for any fenced system the sink itself judged the status vocabulary. My S5 prediction ignored that clause: a prediction miss on the registered contract, recorded as such.
Change: the sink's fence check is revision-only (reject if `fence !== current revision`), the standard fencing-token behaviour. Reason: sink-side status judgement does the system's own job and removes the vocabulary test for every fenced system. This is a contract change made after seeing a result; it is justified by the stated purpose of the fence, not by making S5 pass, and it also moves S1: S1 must still CONFORM (a flip after the read bumps the revision) and S5 now must FAIL at Ta for 6 of 8 forms and hold at Tr1 8 of 8. Every other prediction and the runner falsifier are unchanged. If S1 stops conforming or S5 still conforms, the runner is withdrawn or rebuilt.
