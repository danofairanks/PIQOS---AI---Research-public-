# Benchmark-Validity Readings: Seventeen Papers Read for Whether the Score Supports the Inference Drawn From It

*AI-assisted. Living research: staged, not peer reviewed, may be revised or withdrawn.*

**Scope of naming.** Seventeen papers (eleven posted in October 2026, one in late September 2026, three added on 2026-11-09, one posted in June 2026 and two in November 2026, and two added on 2026-11-10, one first posted in May 2024 and revised through March 2026 and one dated that day) are each read in their own folder, `benchmark_validity_readings_v1/<name>/README.md`. Authors are called "the authors" and are not named; organisations are named only as each paper names them. No intent is attributed to anyone. A thirteenth reading, of a working paper with no stable public copy, is not included (section 7).

**Disclosure.** AI-assisted (Claude, working under this project's own non-default standard: the operator's instructions, preregistration and primary-source checking discipline; not a vanilla configuration). Object selection, method, redaction and release decisions are the operator's. A single reader carried out every reading; no second reader. Where a paper evaluates models from the assisting model's developer or has authors affiliated with it, the individual reading says so, with that framework context. Readings are post hoc unless the folder holds a predictions file.

## 1. What this series is

Papers about whether a benchmark score supports the inference drawn from it: construct validity, dimensionality, differential functioning, saturation, scorer and judge independence, aggregation, contamination. It does not cover studies of how a harness changes scores (one entry, `continual_self_evolving_agents/`, reads such a paper only for whether its metrics support its claim), and it does not cover vendor capability posts.

## 3. Reading lens (applied during reading, not preregistered)

1. Is the construct the benchmark's own claim or the evaluator's? 2. Can the check fail (a null, a preregistered scope)? 3. What does the audited quantity differ from the reported one by? 4. Which alternative explanation does each test fail to separate? 5. Scorer and judge independence from the compared groups. 6. Does the generalization in the abstract match the sample in the limitations? 7. Is the lead larger than the noise implied by task counts, trials and clustering? 8. Does the ranking or headline survive a change in weights, or in which component carries it? 9. Can the headline metric be met by an extreme policy (escalate everything, refuse everything), and is the opposite error reported beside it? 10. Are the compared systems run at matched effort, harness and budget, and is any difference disclosed where the claim is made? 11. Is recall reported with precision, against a compute-matched baseline? (Questions 7-11 were added on 2026-11-09 and applied to the two readings added that day; the earlier twelve were not re-read against them.)

## 4. Evidence tier and deeper-pass flag (convention from 2026-10-25)

Every reading ends with two lines. **Tier** states what it rests on: E1 executed (state what: arithmetic on the paper's reported numbers, a simulation, or the paper's own code or data), E2 read (state what was and was not read), E3 interpretation (name the flags that are interpretation). **Deeper pass** is `recommended`, `optional` or `no`, with the cheapest decisive test and whether it has been done. A deeper pass is recommended only when (a) a flag bears on the paper's headline claim, (b) the code or data needed is public, and (c) the test is small. A reading-only flag that settles the question (a metric defined against its own results) does not need one. Provenance caveat: the papers reached the reader mostly through reposts of abstracts, so counts of flags per reading describe that channel, not arXiv in general. The readings dated 2026-10-25 carry the lines retroactively.

## 5. Reading the flag counts

The number of flags in a reading is not a severity ranking. `raw_rag_agentic_memory/`, `nca_reasoning/` and `mcp_injector_fault_tolerance/` are the contrast cases. The RAW-RAG reading carries as many flags as the readings of papers whose claims outran their tables, but they are mostly scope and reporting detail on a paper that states its own limits (an inherited baseline inconsistency, untested significance, reader dependence). Read a reading by what its flags bear on (the headline claim, or the method's own reporting), not by how many there are.

### 5.1 Patterns that recur (added 2026-11-09; post hoc; seventeen papers and one earlier public reading, not a rate)
Each pattern is listed only where a public reading in this series shows it. Patterns seen once so far are candidates, not findings.
1. **A lead inside the noise, with no intervals.** `pat_paper_assistant_tool/` (26 against 16 of 29 errors), `triage_path_dependence_chatbots/` (a headline exact test on pairs that repeat 28 cases across 13 chatbots), `fde_gym_delivery_benchmark/` (12 of 13 adjacent configuration pairs overlap), `theophyseval_physics_agents/` (a headline resting on a single run), `boxbox_harness_self_revision/` (intervals that resample runs over fixed tasks; a 10.6% lead with no task-level test), `posebench_docking_cofolding/` (run-to-run SD plotted, not test-set sampling; a PoseBusters gap of 3.7 points at n = 130).
2. **A headline carried by one component or one choice.** `ideascientist_ideation_agent/` (the margin comes from an in-domain novelty term), `fde_gym_delivery_benchmark/` (resolved counts dominated by the contact cost), `pairwise_difference_uq/` (a headline quantity defined so that it cannot be negative). Readings of composite indexes whose rank changes with the weights are not in the series yet.
3. **Comparisons run under different conditions.** `raw_rag_agentic_memory/` (the rank claim holds in matched settings and not on one benchmark), `fde_gym_delivery_benchmark/` (budget truncation read as capability), `pat_paper_assistant_tool/` (compute confounded with method), `triage_path_dependence_chatbots/` (sequence confounded with format), `boxbox_harness_self_revision/` (agent control confounded with context preservation, and the compared variant's review cadence close to the method's own).
4. **The evaluator belongs to the builders' loop, or shares a family with the compared systems.** `harmbench_psychometric_audit/` (judge-family confound not discussed), `pat_paper_assistant_tool/` (engine, baseline, grader and audit from one organisation), `triage_path_dependence_chatbots/` (simulator, checklist writer, rewriter and scorer from one developer family, three targets from it too), `boxbox_harness_self_revision/` (benchmark, method, baselines and task filter from one group, the filter model also the agent), `posebench_docking_cofolding/` (the authors define a new dataset variant and a new metric and rank with them), `alphaproof_code_signal_verification/` (the framework's authors run and report the evaluation; mitigated by open-sourcing and the maintainers' feedback), and the public reading `scientisttwo_release_reading_v1.md` (a headline that holds under the in-loop reviewer and not under the held-out one).
5. **A headline number the paper's own tables or definitions do not reproduce.** `pairwise_difference_uq/` (a quantity that cannot be negative against reported negatives), `pat_paper_assistant_tool/` (a "34%" that is 34.5 points, and survey percentages that do not fit the stated cohort sizes), `boxbox_harness_self_revision/` (a per-dollar column 2.4 to 4.7 times above the ratio of the table's own means, reversing the lead), `posebench_docking_cofolding/` (stated test-set sizes of 85 and 122 against scored sizes of 84 and 100).
6. **A single-sided metric with a cheap extreme solution.** `triage_path_dependence_chatbots/`: emergency under-triage can be met by escalating everything, and the best endpoints on it over-triage most. Seen once so far.
7. **Recall without precision.** `pat_paper_assistant_tool/`: no false-positive rate against a compute-matched baseline. Seen once so far.
8. **A per-cost or per-unit metric built as a mean of per-item ratios.** `boxbox_harness_self_revision/`: cheap items dominate, a no-op earns the baseline value, and the ranking reverses on the ratio of means. Seen once so far.
9. **A running maximum over noisy measurements as the score.** `boxbox_harness_self_revision/`: best-so-far speedup with timing variation up to 10% and no re-measurement of the winning snapshot. Seen once so far.
10. **A stated sample size that is not the scored one.** `posebench_docking_cofolding/`: recounting from the released per-complex files gives 84 and 100 scored complexes for sets stated as 85 and 122. Seen once so far; needs released per-item data to detect.
11. **A metric rescaled against the compared set.** `posebench_docking_cofolding/`: an interaction score normalised with the best and worst compared methods, so values change with the roster. Seen once so far.
12. **The baseline set changes between versions of one paper.** `posebench_docking_cofolding/`: a competitive template-based method present in two versions is absent from the one with the stronger headline. Seen once so far.
13. **Conditions disclosed in the body but not carried into the abstract.** `alphaproof_code_signal_verification/` (a harness build, contracts for every cryptographic primitive, post-quantum healing not proved) and `posebench_docking_cofolding/` (the abstract's "comparable ... baseline algorithms" without the input and condition asymmetries stated in the text). Seen twice, in papers whose bodies are otherwise careful; this is a communication-layer pattern, not a defect in the method.
14. **Size of a produced artifact as the headline metric, and cost in labour without resource totals.** `alphaproof_code_signal_verification/`: 6.5 million proof lines; five people for four weeks; no compute, token or dollar totals and no baseline. Seen once so far.
15. **A post-hoc check whose reference may be amended toward the checked artifact.** `alphaproof_code_signal_verification/`: the specification can be amended when proofs fail, under guards; counts and direction of amendments were not in the text read. Seen once so far; needs the released log to test.

## 6. Verification, and what is not claimed

- **Quoted strings.** `quote_checks.py` tests every double-quoted string in each reading against the text layer of the paper's PDF (three extraction orders, normalised quotes and dashes). Seventeen of seventeen readings pass after correction (the five added on 2026-11-09 and 2026-11-10 after one pass of correction each); before correction, many phrases that had been put in quotation marks were close paraphrases, and those were either restored to the paper's wording or unquoted. The result of the final run is in each folder as `quote_checks_output.txt`. A pass is a lexical test: it does not show the reading's interpretation is right.
- **Source files.** Eleven of the twelve arXiv PDFs re-fetched (ten on 2026-10-26, `continual_self_evolving_agents` on 2026-10-29) are byte-identical to the copies read; the twelfth (`nca_reasoning`) differs in bytes but is word-for-word identical in text.
- **Scripts.** Every `checks*.py` re-ran in this layout and reproduced its committed output.
- **Not done.** No paper's code or data was run unless a reading says so; figures were mostly read as extracted text; the flags are leads for a deeper pass, not verdicts.

## 8. Limitations: what this does and does not establish

- Seventeen papers chosen by what reached the reader, not sampled; nothing here is a rate.
- One reader per paper (the assisting model, under this project's non-default standard); in some cases that model's developer is the subject (see each Disclosure).
- The series is a set of reading notes with scripts; it is not a literature review. An open item is a literature table for AI-judging-AI and benchmark-validity work, with inclusion criteria preregistered first. A second open item (added 2026-11-10): whether checks, verification, monitors and filters added after a system is built (post-hoc assurance) share the failure modes recorded here, or whether those modes belong to the genre of abstract and headline compression; a seed set is `alphaproof_code_signal_verification/`, `pat_paper_assistant_tool/`, `mcp_injector_fault_tolerance/` and `harmbench_psychometric_audit/`, and a control set of non-assurance readings would be needed to separate the two, with the coding rule preregistered first. On the single case read so far the modes sat in the abstract and headline, not in the machinery.
